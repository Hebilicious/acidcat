# AcidCat mark explorations

Use case: logo-brand. A horizontal identity exploration sheet with three different mechanical cat head symbols, evenly spaced, all on flat midnight ink. First: elegant cat in right profile, long pointed ears and compact muzzle, lower rear jaw made from an exposed segment of a toothed wheel. Second: a frontal polished brass death mask with acid-green eye lenses set in broad dark sockets. Third: a three-quarter cat made of folded copper facets and a circular temple bearing. Each is a tiny precision instrument maker's hallmark: bold geometry, very few pieces, iconic silhouettes, one acid-green accent per eye, brushed brass color, precise dark counters. Read at 16 pixels. No surrounding badges, text, shadows or labels.

The first profile supplies the silhouette for the SVG. The rear teeth belong to the jaw hinge. The favicon widens the eye and combines the cheek and chin into one plate.
